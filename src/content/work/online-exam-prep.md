## The brief

Students preparing for competitive and academic exams were stuck between two bad options: platforms that were content-heavy but hard to navigate, or test-focused but short on real insight into where they were actually weak. The goal was a single, focused environment — structured practice, timed mock tests, and analytics that mean something — end to end, from first sketch to shipped UI.

## What I built

A modular design system tuned for the problem: a calm, readable type scale and color palette (this is a product people use under time pressure — nothing about it should add anxiety), question cards, timers, progress bars, and charts as reusable components, and explicit patterns for every test state — active, paused, submitted — plus feedback and error handling.

On top of that: a guided onboarding flow based on the student's actual exam goals, a distraction-free test interface with unambiguous timing and navigation, and analytics dashboards built to answer one question clearly — where should I study next.

## Key decisions

Focus-first UI to minimize distraction during study and tests. Real-time feedback that informs without overwhelming. Progress visualization built to motivate continued use, not just report a number. A structure scalable enough to add new subjects and exam formats without a redesign.
