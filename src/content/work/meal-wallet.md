## The brief

Buying a daily healthy meal from Pure Prep's physical stores meant repetitive payment at the counter, no central way to track spending or plans, and long waits at peak hours — with no real bridge between paying digitally and picking up in person. The business side had the same gap: no unified payment system, no retention mechanism, no visibility into how people actually bought.

## What I built

A mobile-first design system rooted in the category — a fresh, nutrition-inspired color system, wallet UI, QR scanner, and card/list components, with explicit states for every transaction outcome (success, failure, loading). On top of that: a frictionless top-up → browse → redeem flow, a QR-based in-store redemption experience simple enough to use one-handed at a counter, and design cues localized to match user expectations in Thailand rather than a generic template.

![Wallet, browse, and redemption — the full flow](/media/agency/meal-wallet.png)

## Key decisions

Wallet-first architecture, since that's the actual anchor of daily use. Minimal taps for repeat purchases — this is a habit product, not a browsing one. A seamless online-to-offline handoff, since the app's only job is to make the physical pickup faster, not to replace it.
